# NBA Technical Requirements — FanDuel VIP KAM Console

**Document type:** Written functional and technical requirements
**Audience:** Salesforce Solutions Architect (SA), Technical Architect (TA)
**Purpose:** Feed into AI-generated intent documents for build planning
**Platform:** Salesforce Sales Console (Lightning), Einstein NBA / Strategy Builder, Agentforce
**Last updated:** September 2026

---

## 1. Overview

The Next Best Action (NBA) queue is one of the primary intelligence surfaces in the FanDuel VIP KAM Console. Its purpose is to replace the current ad hoc process — where KAMs manually review spreadsheets, Slack alerts, and email chains to identify which VIPs need attention — with a prioritized, AI-scored feed of actionable recommendations surfaced directly in the Salesforce console.

In Salesforce terms, NBA is delivered through Einstein Recommendation Builder and Strategy Builder. The SA will configure one or more Recommendation Strategies — sets of branching logic and scoring rules — that evaluate VIP data and produce ranked recommendation cards. Each card represents a specific scenario (no contact, CS interaction, big loss, etc.), carries contextual data fields, and offers the KAM a set of actions (Accept, Decline, or Defer). Cards are surfaced via the standard `recommendationAssistant` Lightning component embedded in the console's activity column, or via a custom LWC that wraps the same underlying strategy output.

The Agentforce AI layer enhances cards in Phase 3. For MVP (Phases 1–2), cards are rules-based and static — they fire on threshold conditions and display structured data. In Phase 3, each card type gains an Agentforce-generated insight block: talking points, recommended messaging, churn risk scoring, or bonus ROI calculation. This is delivered by the Agentforce KAM Companion, which can also surface NBA-driven recommendations on demand through its chat interface.

---

## 2. Architecture Summary

The NBA component occupies the middle column of the three-column Sales Console layout, positioned below the workspace tab bar and above the Tasks and WPC widget. It is always visible and renders up to 5 collapsed cards by default, with a "View all" affordance for the full queue.

At the Salesforce platform level, each NBA scenario maps to one or more Recommendation records generated by a Strategy. A Strategy in Einstein Recommendation Builder is a declarative logic tree: it evaluates conditions (field values, related record counts, date calculations), scores each candidate recommendation, filters and ranks them, and emits the top N cards to the component. The Strategy runs either on page load (synchronous evaluation) or via a scheduled job that pre-calculates the queue and stores results in a custom object for fast retrieval — the TA should determine which approach fits the data volume and latency requirements.

Each card emits an Accept, Decline, or Defer response from the KAM. These responses are stored as Recommendation Reaction records in Salesforce, which feeds back into the Strategy's scoring and suppression logic. For example, a Declined card for a specific VIP should not reappear for a configurable suppression window (default: 48 hours). An Accepted card should trigger a downstream action — a Task creation, a log entry, or a Flow invocation — depending on the scenario.

In Phase 3, the Agentforce KAM Companion is wired into the NBA component so that accepting a card can invoke a Companion prompt pre-loaded with the VIP's context. The companion returns a suggested message, talking points, or bonus recommendation in the chat panel, which the KAM can refine and act on.

## 3. Scenario Requirements

### Scenario 1 — No Contact Alert

**Purpose:** Surface VIPs who have not received KAM outreach within their tier-appropriate contact cadence, enabling proactive relationship maintenance before engagement deteriorates.

**Trigger & Logic**

The strategy should evaluate every VIP in the KAM's book of business daily. For each VIP, it calculates the number of calendar days elapsed since the most recent Activity record where the record type is one of: Task (completed), Event (completed), EmailMessage (sent), or LoggedCall. SMS contact will be included once SnippetSentry integration is live (Q4, not MVP).

The threshold that triggers a card is determined first by the VIP's GGR tier, then optionally overridden by their primary product line:

- Top 10% GGR tier: card fires after 7 days without contact
- 11–25% GGR tier: card fires after 14 days
- 26–75% GGR tier: card fires after 21 days
- Remaining: card fires after 28 days
- Product override (if product-specific config is enabled): Sportsbook default = 14 days, Casino default = 30 days. The product override takes precedence over the tier threshold when active.

Severity is assigned based on how far the VIP has exceeded their threshold, not on absolute days:

- 0–5 days over threshold = Orange
- 6–15 days over threshold = Yellow
- 16+ days over threshold = Red

The default console view should display only Red-severity cards to reduce noise. Orange and Yellow cards should be accessible via a severity filter toggle on the component.

**Card Content Requirements**

Each card must display: VIP Name (linked to Contact record), FanDuel ID, Last Contact Date, Days Since Contact (as a numeric with severity color applied), Contact Method used in last interaction, a one-line Communication Summary pulled from the last Activity subject/description, Player Tier, RTC % (current vs. target), Last Wager Date, and 30-Day Play Trend vs. Average (displayed as a directional indicator: up/flat/down with % delta).

**KAM Actions**

- **Contact Now** (Accept): Creates a Task record pre-populated with "Outreach — No Contact Alert" and opens the Log Contact modal. The card is suppressed for that VIP for 24 hours after the task is created.
- **Defer** : Snoozes the card for a configurable period (default: 24 hours; KAM selects from 24h, 48h, 72h). The card reappears after the snooze window unless contact has been logged.
- **Dismiss** (Decline): Removes the card from the queue. The KAM must provide a dismiss reason from a picklist (e.g., "Future contact scheduled," "VIP inactive," "Do not surface"). The card does not re-fire for 7 days.

**Agentforce AI Layer (Phase 3)**

When the KAM opens a No Contact Alert card, the Agentforce KAM Companion generates: (1) a suggested contact method based on the VIP's historical response patterns (e.g., "Ethan responds fastest to SMS on weekday mornings"), (2) 2–3 personalized talking points derived from the VIP's recent wager activity and preferences, and (3) a recommended optimal contact window based on historical activity patterns. Cards for "Do Not Surface" accounts and those with future touchpoints already scheduled are automatically filtered out by the Strategy before they reach the KAM.

**Data Requirements**

The following must be present in Salesforce for this scenario to function: Activity records (Tasks, Events, EmailMessages) linked to VIP Contact/Account records with accurate completion dates. A GGR Tier field on the Contact or Account object, populated from Data Cloud or ACE. An RTC % field (actual) and RTC Target field. A Last Wager Date field and a 30-Day Play Trend field (calculated or synced from Data Cloud). A "Do Not Surface" flag on the Contact record. A Product Primary field (SBK/CAS/DFS/FDR) to apply product-specific thresholds.

**Open Questions**

1. Are Activity records currently being created in Salesforce for all KAM contact methods (email, call, SMS)? Is there a gap where contact is logged outside Salesforce (e.g., direct email not tracked)?
2. Is GGR Tier a field that updates in real time, or is it a weekly/monthly batch update? The threshold logic depends on its freshness.
3. Should the product-specific threshold override be configurable by the SA in Custom Metadata, or does FanDuel require a UI for KAMs/managers to adjust thresholds without a deployment?

**Build Phase:** MVP (Phase 1–2). All required data fields exist on the Contact/Account object or can be configured without a Data Cloud dependency. SMS tracking excluded from MVP.

### Scenario 2 — CS/HVP Interaction Alert

**Purpose:** Notify the KAM when a VIP has contacted Customer Service or the High Value Player team so the KAM can maintain relationship continuity and address issues before they escalate.

**Trigger & Logic**

The strategy evaluates open and recently closed Cases where the Contact (VIP) is linked to a Case created within the prior 24 hours. The Case must have a record type or category that identifies it as originating from a CS or HVP team interaction — this classification needs to be defined and enforced by the CS/HVP teams in their Case creation workflow. The 24-hour window is a rolling check: a card fires when a new qualifying Case is created, and remains active until the KAM takes action or the Case is closed with no follow-up flagged.

If a VIP has more than one open Case within the 24-hour window, a single card fires with a multi-case flag rather than separate cards per case.

**Card Content Requirements**

Each card must display: Client Name (linked to Contact), FanDuel ID, Case Status (Open/Closed), CS or HVP Representative name (assigned user on the Case), Case Subject (the issue type), Interaction Summary (a brief from the Case description or comments), and a multiple-cases flag (e.g., "3 cases open") when applicable.

**Color/Severity Logic**

Cards for open Cases are styled with a Red severity indicator. Cards for Cases closed within the 24-hour window are styled Green. If a Case transitions from Open to Closed after the card has fired, the card should refresh to reflect the updated status.

**KAM Actions**

- **Contact VIP** (Accept): Opens the Log Contact modal pre-populated with the Case reference. Creates a Task linked to both the Contact and the Case.
- **Engage CS/HVP** (secondary action): Opens a Chatter message draft addressed to the Case owner.
- **Defer**: Snoozes for 4 hours (the short window reflects urgency). Configurable.
- **Dismiss**: Requires a dismiss reason. Available only after the Case is closed.

**Agentforce AI Layer (Phase 3)**

For closed Cases, Agentforce generates an executive summary of the interaction with sentiment analysis (positive/neutral/negative) and a flag if follow-up action is recommended based on the issue type and resolution outcome. For open Cases, Agentforce recommends whether the KAM should contact the VIP directly or engage the CS/HVP team member first, and suggests appropriate messaging calibrated to issue severity and the VIP's tier. In both cases, Agentforce identifies if the VIP has a pattern of recurring issues and surfaces that context.

**Data Requirements**

This scenario is primarily a **process and people dependency**, not a technology constraint. For the trigger to work, CS and HVP teams must create Cases in Salesforce for every VIP interaction — this cannot be inferred or derived from another system. The SA must confirm with FanDuel operations whether this practice is in place today or whether it requires a process change and adoption program. Additionally, Cases must be linked to the VIP's Contact record (not just an Account), and the Case record type must include a field that distinguishes CS-originated from HVP-originated interactions. Near-real-time Case creation is assumed — a batch sync would make the 24-hour trigger unreliable.

**Open Questions**

1. Do CS and HVP teams currently create Cases in Salesforce for VIP interactions? Is this a current behavior or a future-state requirement?
2. Is there a defined Case record type taxonomy that distinguishes VIP CS cases from standard cases? If not, how should the Strategy filter to only relevant cases?
3. If a Case is created by HVP but the KAM is not the VIP's assigned KAM (e.g., the VIP called in and was handled by a general HVP team member), should the card still fire for the KAM?

**Build Phase:** MVP (Phase 1–2), contingent on confirmation that Cases are being created in Salesforce by CS/HVP teams. If Cases are not being created, this scenario must be deferred until the process is established.

### Scenario 3 — Big Losses Alert

**Purpose:** Create immediate KAM awareness when a VIP experiences a significant loss so that the KAM can engage empathetically and proactively before the VIP disengages.

**Trigger & Logic**

This scenario has two distinct trigger categories and they should be implemented as two separate card subtypes within the same scenario grouping:

**Category A — Absolute Loss Threshold (Red):** A card fires when a VIP's single-day net loss exceeds $100,000. This threshold is based on the total loss across all bets settled on a given calendar day, not any single bet in isolation. The card fires on the day the threshold is crossed, based on data received from the Player Intelligence Engine feed.

**Category B — Relative Loss Anomaly (Orange):** A card fires when a VIP's loss on a given day exceeds 5x their 30-day average daily handle. This is a relative threshold intended to catch significant loss events for players whose typical stake is lower — a $5,000 loss may be unremarkable for a high-volume player but alarming for someone who typically wagers $500 per day. The calculation requires the L30 average handle to be available as a field or derived metric in Salesforce.

Both categories require the Player Intelligence Engine data from FanDuel to be present in Salesforce — either pushed via the AWS Agent integration or synced through Salesforce Data Cloud. This data is not available in MVP.

**Card Content Requirements**

Category A card must display: VIP Name, FanDuel ID, Loss Amount (prominently displayed), Bet ID(s) associated with the loss, a summary of Wager Details (sport, bet type, stake), and an indicator of whether the loss is from a single large bet or cumulative across multiple bets settled on the same day.

Category B card must display: VIP Name, FanDuel ID, Yesterday's Total Handle, L30 Average Handle, Number of Bets placed yesterday, Total Loss amount, and % Variance from the player's normal handle.

**Color/Severity Logic**

Category A cards = Red (absolute loss threshold, high urgency). Category B cards = Orange (relative anomaly, moderate urgency). Within Category A, an optional escalation to a "Critical" state (e.g., dark red or pulsing indicator) can be added for losses exceeding $500K, but this is a Phase 3 refinement.

**KAM Actions**

- **Plan Outreach** (Accept): Triggers an Agentforce prompt pre-loaded with the loss context and suggests a timing recommendation (immediate vs. 24-hour cooling period). Creates a follow-up Task.
- **Flag for RG Review** (secondary): Creates a Responsible Gaming flag record linked to the VIP. Required if Agentforce identifies potential problem gambling indicators.
- **Defer**: 24-hour snooze. The card re-fires at the 24-hour mark with a "Revisit" label if no contact has been logged.
- **Dismiss**: Only available after contact is logged or the VIP is explicitly flagged as not requiring follow-up. Requires a reason.

**Agentforce AI Layer (Phase 3)**

When this card fires, Agentforce assesses whether the loss is within the player's typical risk tolerance by comparing it to their historical win/loss pattern. It generates an empathy-based outreach message calibrated to the severity and the player's relationship history. It recommends a contact timing strategy — whether to reach out immediately or allow a cooling period — based on the player's known communication preferences and past reactions to similar events. If the loss pattern suggests potential problem gambling behavior, Agentforce flags the card for Responsible Gaming review with a specific recommendation.

**Data Requirements**

This scenario requires: daily bet settlement data per VIP (win/loss amounts, bet IDs, wager details) from the FanDuel Player Intelligence Engine. This data must be available in Salesforce, either as a custom object populated by the AWS Agent data feed or as a Data Cloud segment pushed to Salesforce. The L30 average handle field for Category B must be computed and stored, not calculated on the fly during strategy evaluation (for performance). High-severity play indicators as defined by FanDuel's internal risk classification should also be available.

**Open Questions**

1. What is the agreed data delivery mechanism for Player Intelligence Engine data into Salesforce? AWS Agent push to a custom object, or Data Cloud sync? What is the expected latency (real-time, hourly, end-of-day)?
2. Are FanDuel's Responsible Gaming (RG) review workflows already in Salesforce? If so, what is the record type or object used for RG flags, so the "Flag for RG Review" action can write to it correctly?
3. Is the $100K threshold configurable per KAM book or is it a global setting? Should Shield Select VIPs have a lower threshold given their higher typical play volume?

**Build Phase:** Phase 3. This scenario cannot be built in MVP without confirmed data availability from the Player Intelligence Engine. The prototype will show mock cards for demonstration purposes.

### Scenario 4 — Show Some Love (Bonus Opportunity)

**Purpose:** Identify VIPs who have not received a KAM-issued bonus recently and whose RTC is below target, surfacing a targeted bonus opportunity to improve retention and play frequency.

**Trigger & Logic**

A card fires when both of the following conditions are true simultaneously for a VIP: (1) the date of the last KAM-issued bonus exceeds 15 days ago, and (2) the VIP's current RTC percentage is below their RTC target. Both fields must be available on the Contact or Account record. The strategy should evaluate this daily for all VIPs in the KAM's book.

It is important to note that the bonus field used for the trigger must specifically reflect KAM-issued generosity bonuses, not system-generated promotional bonuses. If the data model does not distinguish between these two types, the trigger will fire inaccurately for VIPs who have received system promos but no personal KAM outreach. This distinction must be resolved in the data model before build.

**Card Content Requirements**

Each card must display: VIP Name (linked to Contact), FanDuel ID, Last Bonus Date, Last Bonus Amount, Last Bonus Type, L7 GGR, L7 Handle, Most Recent Event attended or invited to, and Days Since Last KAM Bonus (numeric, color-coded: 15–29 days = Orange, 30+ days = Red).

**Color/Severity Logic**

Severity is driven primarily by how far below target the VIP's RTC is, combined with days since last bonus. A VIP who is significantly below RTC target and 30+ days without a bonus is higher priority than one who is slightly below target and 16 days without a bonus. The Strategy scoring should weight RTC delta (target minus actual) at approximately 60% and days since bonus at 40%, producing a ranked queue.

**KAM Actions**

- **Issue Bonus** (Accept): Opens the Bonus Issuance flow (or navigates to the ACE bonus workflow, depending on where bonus issuance is executed). Pre-populates the VIP record reference. Creates a Task to track the bonus issuance.
- **View Promo Options** (secondary): Opens a sidebar or modal showing available promotions the VIP has not yet received, sourced from ACE.
- **Defer**: Snoozes for 48 hours.
- **Dismiss**: Requires a reason from a picklist (e.g., "Bonus not appropriate at this time," "VIP recently declined offer," "Under commercial review").

**Agentforce AI Layer (Phase 3)**

Agentforce proposes a specific bonus amount and type based on the VIP's tier, play patterns, and historical bonus response rates — including which bonus types have historically driven the highest re-engagement. It identifies available promotional offers the VIP has not opted into and highlights those with the highest predicted conversion probability given the VIP's betting preferences. It generates personalized messaging to accompany the bonus, referencing the VIP's favorite sport, recent activity, or upcoming events. Finally, it calculates an expected ROI for the proposed bonus based on predicted handle uplift.

**Data Requirements**

The following must be available in Salesforce: Last KAM Bonus Date and Amount fields on Contact/Account, clearly distinguished from system-generated promos (requires ACE integration or a custom object for bonus history). RTC Actual % and RTC Target % fields, refreshed at a frequency appropriate for the business cadence (daily minimum). L7 GGR and L7 Handle fields from Data Cloud or ACE. Available promotional offers linked to each VIP — this requires a data model that makes ACE promo availability queryable from Salesforce.

**Open Questions**

1. Is there a confirmed integration plan between ACE and Salesforce for bonus history and promo availability? Is this part of the Data Cloud roadmap or a separate API integration?
2. How does the current data model distinguish a KAM-issued bonus from a system-generated promo? Is this a field value, a record type, or a relationship to a separate object?
3. What is the approval process for KAM-issued bonuses above a certain threshold? Does the Bonus Issuance flow need to include an approval step, and if so, what is the approval hierarchy?

**Build Phase:** MVP shell (Phase 1–2 with static mock data in prototype). Full build in Phase 2 once RTC and bonus data are confirmed in Salesforce. Agentforce recommendation layer in Phase 3.

### Scenario 5 — Status Change Alert

**Purpose:** Alert KAMs to VIP account status changes so they can intervene immediately for at-risk accounts and evaluate re-engagement opportunities for reactivated players.

**Trigger & Logic**

This scenario uses two distinct trigger directions, each with different timing requirements:

**Live → Non-Live transition:** A card fires on the same day the status field on the VIP's Contact or Account record changes from any "Live" value to any "Non-Live" value. The strategy should use a Record-Triggered Flow or a Platform Event to detect this change in near-real-time, rather than a scheduled daily evaluation. For high-value VIPs (Shield Select tier and Tier 1), the card should be elevated to the top of the NBA queue regardless of other scoring.

**Non-Live → Live transition:** A card fires 72 hours after the status field changes from a "Non-Live" value to a "Live" value. The 72-hour delay is intentional — it allows time to assess whether the reactivation represents genuine re-engagement or a single session before the KAM invests in a bonus or outreach. A Scheduled Flow triggered at the time of the status change, with a 72-hour wait step, is the recommended implementation pattern.

**Card Content Requirements**

Each card must display: VIP Name, FanDuel ID, New Status (prominently labeled), Previous Status, Change Reason (from a defined picklist on the status change record or field history), a free-text Description field for context, and Date and Time of the Status Change. For the Non-Live → Live direction, the card should also show a brief summary of the VIP's play activity in the 72 hours since reactivation (total handle, number of sessions).

**Color/Severity Logic**

Live → Non-Live cards = Red for Shield Select and Tier 1 VIPs; Orange for all other tiers. Non-Live → Live cards = Blue (opportunity, not risk).

**KAM Actions**

For Live → Non-Live: **Reach Out** (Accept) creates an urgent Task and suggests a win-back approach. **Enroll in Win-Back Campaign** (secondary) triggers a Campaign enrollment Flow. **Defer** (24 hours). **Dismiss** with reason.

For Non-Live → Live: **Evaluate Bonus** (Accept) opens the bonus consideration flow pre-populated with 72-hour play data. **Send Welcome Back** (secondary) generates a personalized message template. **Defer** (48 hours — the window before the re-engagement window closes). **Dismiss** with reason.

**Agentforce AI Layer (Phase 3)**

For Live → Non-Live: Agentforce identifies the probable cause of the status change (e.g., recent losing streak, withdrawal pattern, CS complaint history) and recommends an outreach strategy scaled to the VIP's tier and lifetime value. It suggests win-back messaging and evaluates whether enrolling the VIP in an existing win-back campaign is appropriate. For Non-Live → Live: Agentforce calculates an optimal bonus recommendation based on the VIP's 72-hour play activity and predicts the likelihood of sustained engagement. It also surfaces accounts with frequent status oscillation and flags them for deeper review, providing a historical status timeline.

**Data Requirements**

Status field on Contact or Account with a defined picklist of statuses, clearly categorized as Live or Non-Live values. A Change Reason field, ideally captured at the point of status change via a validation rule or required field flow. Field history tracking enabled on the status field (or a custom Status Change object that logs transitions). For the 72-hour Non-Live → Live card, a lightweight record of the status change timestamp is required to calculate when to fire the scheduled flow. Historical status change data with reasons is required for the Agentforce frequency-oscillation analysis in Phase 3.

**Open Questions**

1. Are "Live" and "Non-Live" the actual status values used in FanDuel's Salesforce instance, or are there more granular status values (e.g., Active, Restricted, Self-Excluded, Dormant)? The trigger logic must map to the actual field values.
2. Is the Change Reason currently captured when a status changes? If not, adding it as a required field on the status change flow will require a process change.
3. For the real-time Live → Non-Live trigger, is a Platform Event or Record-Triggered Flow the preferred implementation approach given the org's current governor limit headroom and any existing automation on the status field?

**Build Phase:** MVP (Phase 1–2). Status change tracking using native Salesforce Field History and Flow is fully achievable without external data dependencies.

### Scenario 6 — Week-over-Week Handle Decline Alert

**Purpose:** Provide early warning of declining VIP engagement before the player appears on the formal Weekly Player Concerns (WPC) report, allowing proactive intervention at the first sign of disengagement.

**Trigger & Logic**

A card fires when a VIP's total handle for the most recently completed calendar week is 20% or more below their handle for the prior week. The comparison is week-over-week (Mon–Sun), evaluated once per week (Monday morning run recommended, using prior week data). The strategy must also track consecutive weeks of decline — if a VIP has declined 20%+ for two or more consecutive weeks, the card severity increases regardless of the current percentage.

Severity thresholds: 20–30% WoW decline = Yellow. 31–40% = Orange. 40%+ = Red. Two or more consecutive weeks of 20%+ decline automatically elevates to Orange regardless of the current percentage.

**Card Content Requirements**

Each card must display: VIP Name, FanDuel ID, Prior Week Handle (dollar amount), Current Week Handle, WoW % Decrease (displayed prominently with severity color), Average Handle (L12-week rolling average, for context), and Consecutive Weeks of Decline (if greater than 1).

**Color/Severity Logic**

As defined in the trigger logic above. Consecutive weeks modifier applies on top of the single-week threshold. The component should visually distinguish between a single-week dip (which may be noise) and a multi-week trend (which requires intervention).

**KAM Actions**

- **Plan Engagement** (Accept): Creates a Task and opens the Agentforce companion for outreach suggestions.
- **Review WPC Report** (secondary link): Deep-links to the WPC report filtered to this VIP.
- **Defer**: 7-day snooze (weekly cadence appropriate given weekly trigger).
- **Dismiss**: Requires a reason. Dismissed cards re-evaluate the following week.

**Agentforce AI Layer (Phase 3)**

Agentforce analyzes the decline in the context of the sports calendar and the VIP's betting preferences — for example, identifying that a tennis-focused player had no major ATP events the prior week, or that a player's primary team is on a bye week. It predicts whether the decline is a temporary fluctuation or the beginning of a sustained trend based on historical volatility patterns. It surfaces upcoming events and matchups relevant to the VIP's interests as potential re-engagement hooks. It also flags if the decline correlates with competitive activity or broader market factors, and recommends a threshold for intervention calibrated to the VIP's typical handle volatility.

**Data Requirements**

Weekly handle totals per VIP must be available in Salesforce, either as a custom object populated by a Data Cloud sync or a scheduled data import from the Player Intelligence Engine. A 12-week rolling average handle field is required (calculated or pre-computed). Consecutive weeks of decline is ideally tracked as a custom field updated by the weekly batch process, rather than calculated in real-time during Strategy evaluation. Sports calendar and event data integration is required for the Agentforce analysis layer in Phase 3 — this is an additional data dependency beyond what is needed for the MVP rules-based card.

**Open Questions**

1. Is weekly handle data currently available in Salesforce, or would this require a new data pipeline from the Player Intelligence Engine / Data Cloud?
2. How is "handle" defined for multi-product VIPs — is it the sum across all products (SBK + CAS + DFS + Racing), or is WoW decline evaluated per product line separately?
3. Should KAMs be able to configure per-VIP thresholds (e.g., exempt a VIP from WoW alerts during known low-activity seasons), or is this a global configuration only?

**Build Phase:** Phase 3. Requires historical weekly handle data in Salesforce. Can be mocked in prototype. The rules-based card can be built in Phase 2 if the data pipeline is confirmed, but Agentforce sports calendar analysis is Phase 3.

## 4. Cross-Cutting Technical Requirements

### Suppress / Do Not Surface

The Strategy must evaluate a "Do Not Surface" flag on every VIP Contact record before firing any card for that VIP. If this flag is true, no NBA cards should fire for that VIP regardless of scenario conditions. This flag should be settable by the KAM, their Manager, or an administrator, and should include a reason and an expiry date (after which the flag is automatically cleared). VIPs with a future-dated Task or Event scheduled within 48 hours should also be suppressed from No Contact Alert cards, but remain eligible for other card types (e.g., Big Loss).

### Dismiss, Defer, and Snooze

Dismiss and Defer are native capabilities of Salesforce Recommendation Reactions. The build team should use the native reaction mechanism rather than building a custom suppress object, unless the native mechanism cannot support the scenario-specific suppress windows. Dismiss should write a reaction record with a reason. Defer should write a reaction record with a resume timestamp. The Strategy must check for active reaction records before surfacing any card. Dismiss reason picklists should be defined per scenario and stored in Custom Metadata for easy administration.

### Card Priority Scoring

When multiple cards fire simultaneously for the same KAM, the strategy must rank them. The default scoring model should weight by: (1) scenario urgency — Big Loss Alert and Status Change (Live → Non-Live) are highest priority; CS Interaction and No Contact (Red) are second; Bonus Opportunity and Status Change (Non-Live → Live) are third; WoW Decline is fourth; (2) VIP tier — Shield Select cards score above Tier 1, which score above Tier 2–3; (3) days overdue — within the same scenario, cards that have been active longer score higher until dismissed. The SA should implement this as a scoring formula in the Strategy and expose the weights as configurable Custom Metadata records so they can be tuned without a deployment.

### Role-Based Visibility

A KAM should see NBA cards only for VIPs in their own book of business — defined by the KAM's User record being the Assigned KAM on the VIP's Contact or Account. A Manager should see cards for all VIPs assigned to KAMs in their team (one level down in the user hierarchy). A Director should see cards for their entire division. This scoping must be implemented at the Strategy level using the running user's role and team membership, not as a sharing rule, to ensure the Strategy does not return records the user cannot act on.

### Deduplication

A card for a given VIP in a given scenario should not fire more than once per triggering event. For event-based triggers (CS case creation, status change, big loss), the Strategy must store a reference to the triggering record and suppress duplicate cards tied to the same record ID. For threshold-based triggers (no contact, WoW decline, bonus), the daily evaluation should check whether an active (non-dismissed, non-snoozed) card for that VIP and scenario already exists before creating a new one.

## 6. Open Scoping Questions for the SA

1. **Activity logging discipline:** Are KAMs currently logging all contact activity (calls, emails, SMS, in-person) in Salesforce? The effectiveness of Scenario 1 (No Contact Alert) depends entirely on this. If contact is being logged outside Salesforce, what is the path to consolidating it?

2. **CS/HVP Case creation:** Do the Customer Service and High Value Player teams create Salesforce Cases for every VIP interaction today? If not, this is a process change that must happen before Scenario 2 can be built, and it requires stakeholder buy-in from those teams.

3. **ACE integration scope and timeline:** Several scenarios (4, and partially 1) depend on data from ACE. What is the confirmed integration approach — direct Salesforce API connection, Data Cloud ingestion, or manual export/import? What is the implementation timeline?

4. **AWS Agent / Player Intelligence Engine data feed:** Scenarios 3 and 6 require real-time or daily play data from FanDuel's Player Intelligence Engine. Has the data engineering team confirmed the delivery mechanism (push vs. pull, frequency, format)? Is this on the Data Cloud roadmap?

5. **Status field values and taxonomy:** What are the exact status field values used in the FanDuel Salesforce instance for VIP account status? Are "Live" and "Non-Live" the actual values or are there more granular states? The trigger logic for Scenario 5 must map to real field values.

6. **KAM-issued bonus vs. system-generated promo distinction:** Is there currently a reliable way in the data model to distinguish a bonus issued personally by a KAM from a system-generated promotional bonus? This is the key data quality question for Scenario 4's trigger accuracy.

7. **Approval workflows for bonus issuance:** When a KAM accepts a "Show Some Love" card and initiates a bonus, does that action require manager approval? If so, what are the approval thresholds and the approval chain?

8. **Responsible Gaming integration:** Scenarios 3 and 6 both have potential RG flagging actions. Does a Responsible Gaming case management workflow currently exist in Salesforce? If so, what is the object and record type so the NBA action can write to it? If not, what is the agreed escalation path?

9. **Strategy Builder vs. custom LWC:** For the MVP build, is the preference to use the native Einstein Recommendation Builder and `recommendationAssistant` component (lower development effort, less visual customization), or to build a custom LWC that queries recommendation records (more effort, but allows full visual control matching the prototype design)? This decision affects both Phase 1 timeline and the long-term maintainability of the component.

10. **Data refresh frequency:** Several fields (GGR Tier, RTC %, handle data) need to be refreshed regularly for the strategy conditions to be accurate. What is the agreed data refresh cadence for each data source, and does that cadence meet the business need for each scenario?
